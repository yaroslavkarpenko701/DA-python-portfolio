# DA-python-portfolio

The main goal is to clean and analyze the data and identify valuable insights into the company's operations.

**Project stages**:

1. Data overview
  * The dataset was loaded, and the contents of the columns were analyzed and described. The key fields that link the three tables were identified.
2. Data cleaning
  * The work was done with missing data, incorrect data, and anomalies.
3. Data analysis and visualization
  * Three tables were joined into a single data frame. The data frame was normalized.
  * The total number of orders, total revenue, and total number of countries covered were determined.
  * Sales (revenue, expenses, profit, product popularity) were analyzed, and corresponding visualizations were created broken down by product categories, geography, and sales channels.
  * The time interval between an order and its shipment was analyzed, and corresponding visualizations were created by product category, country, and region
  * An analysis was conducted to determine whether profit depends on the time required to ship a product. The necessary aggregations and visualizations were created for this purpose.
  * Sales trends (over time) were analyzed using visualizations by product category, country, and region, and key trends were identified.
  * An analysis of product sales by day of the week was conducted. Products were examined to determine whether they are seasonal.
4.Reporting
  * General conclusions and results from a business logic point of view.
